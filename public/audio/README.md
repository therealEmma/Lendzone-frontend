# Lendzone music

Add your own music files in this folder:

- `lobby.mp3` — looping music for the lobby and waiting room.
- `combat.mp3` — looping music for the live round.
- `Gunshot.mp3` — the shot sample used for every local and remote gunshot.

The game starts the lobby track when the player clicks **ENTER THE ARENA** and switches to the combat track when the round begins. These files are optional; the built-in shooting, hit, reload, jump, boost, and countdown SFX remain active.
